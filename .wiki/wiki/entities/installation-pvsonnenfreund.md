# Installation PVSonnenfreund

An [SH10RT-V112](device-sh-rt.md) on [P023](firmware-sapphire-h-b001-v000-p023.md) with an
[SBR096](device-sbr-battery.md), three modules of 20 cells, BCU `SBRBCU-S_22011.01.30`, read by the
owner over the inverter's own LAN port at unit 200. No diagnosis yet; the owner's own block reads
confirm the per-module cell extremes and report two mismatched frames past the cell block
([issue #788](../sources/pvsonnenfreund-sbr096-cell-registers-788-summary.md)).
