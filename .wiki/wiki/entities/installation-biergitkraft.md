# Installation BiergitKraft

An [SH10T](device-sh-t.md) on [`PEARL-H_B001.V001.P038`](firmware-pearl-h-b001-v001-p038.md) with an
[SBH100](device-sbh-battery.md) and a DTSU666-20 meter, read through a
[WiNet-S](device-winet-s.md)2 on Ethernet (issue #792). One reading.

- **The first SH10T and the first B001 firmware on the T** in the corpus.
- The dongle refused what a WiNet-S refuses (6100, the history, the meter's phase voltages and
  currents); every block of the poll answered, 33149-33150 included.
- The capability test did not run: the inverter was in Standby after dark, the battery at its
  15 % floor.
