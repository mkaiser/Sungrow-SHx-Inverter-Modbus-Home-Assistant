## Concepts

- [A million kWh is not a reading](concepts/a-million-kwh-is-not-a-reading.md) — A broken high word reads as a number, so a lifetime counter is refused above a million kWh, and a counter one wrap short is caught by what it has to contain #decoding #counters #recorder #firmware
- [Contention](concepts/contention.md) — Few sessions, 90 s to recover; progressive failure means contention, a fixed address means a fault #modbus #sessions #polling
- [The capability test's restore](concepts/capability-test-restore.md) — Snapshot before the first write, raw-word restore, never skipped; exit 5 means a house is left changed #capability-test #restore #safety
- [Energy history (inputs 6227-6637)](concepts/energy-history.md) — Monthly U16 and yearly U32 totals from 2015, cable only; the yearly slot is the sum of the months at four houses, not at gerd #energy-history #cable-only #registers
- [The capability test](concepts/capability-test.md) — Part B: three legs (plumbing, scale, behaviour) and the rules the procedure turns on #capability-test #writes #scale #diagnosis
- [A generator the inverter cannot see](concepts/external-generator.md) — A non-Sungrow inverter makes load_power low; external.py adds it back #load-power #external #options
- [A failed read is not a refusal](concepts/failed-read-is-not-refusal.md) — Exception responses are answers, link failures are not; probes are three-valued #modbus #probes #evidence
- [Firmware gating](concepts/firmware-gating.md) — Firmware is read first; ABSENT from refusals, CONFIRMED only from a watched write #firmware #capabilities #writes
- [Identify by what answers](concepts/identify-by-what-answers.md) — Sweep unit ids, never trust a port; the unit table by device and transport #discovery #unit-id #config-flow
- [Adopting the legacy entity ids](concepts/legacy-id-adoption.md) — Claim the YAML entity_id at creation so history continues; renames are refused onto known ids #migration #history #entity-id #recorder
- [The legacy slot of a second inverter](concepts/legacy-slot.md) — Which inverter was inv n is measured from the serial sensor; None is not the empty slot #migration #history #multi-inverter
- [A managed wallbox's phases are the inverter's](concepts/managed-wallbox-phases.md) — Automatic only, by power: one phase in PV surplus without grid purchase; no setting, and 21204 does not do it #wallbox #phases #ems
- [Padded frames](concepts/padded-frames.md) — 2612 and 2628 send 15-register frames; layout.COUNTS and ISOLATE, measured with a strict client #modbus #layout #block-read
- [Protocol addresses and register numbers](concepts/protocol-addresses.md) — Addresses are one below register numbers; maps use reg(), no literal addresses #addresses #registers #convention
- [A remote site's link drops](concepts/remote-link-loss.md) — The provider cut gerd's line overnight; the tunnel did not recover until restarted; an up interface says nothing #remote #vpn #polling #evidence
- [Serial anonymization](concepts/serial-anonymization.md) — Stand-ins in every published document, transcripts never tracked, the tree checked by shape #privacy #serial #diagnosis
- [An mDNS browse is a floor, not a census](concepts/mdns-browse-is-a-floor.md) — A record heard is a fact, a record missing is not; a declined firewall prompt leaves services_heard non-zero and the browse short, by the same eight records every time #mdns #discovery #firewall
- [Telling a proxy from the inverter](concepts/telling-a-proxy-from-the-inverter.md) — A proxy forwards every register; the MAC's U/L bit is what decides, and only on the segment #proxy #discovery #mac #transport
- [Transport detection](concepts/transport-detection.md) — 6100 decides cable versus dongle; what differs by transport #transport #winet-s #capabilities
- [Zero and empty mean absent](concepts/zero-means-absent.md) — A dongle's 0 and a NUL-filled string are not evidence of a capability #capabilities #winet-s #decoding
