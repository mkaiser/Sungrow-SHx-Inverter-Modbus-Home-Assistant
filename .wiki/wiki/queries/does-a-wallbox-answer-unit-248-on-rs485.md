# Does a wallbox answer unit 248 on RS485?

**Open.** Others report that a [wallbox](../entities/device-wallbox.md)'s
RS485 interface answers at unit 248. That has never been measured here. What
is measured is unit 248 over **TLS on port 516**, at its own LAN address, at
[gerd](../entities/installation-gerd.md).
