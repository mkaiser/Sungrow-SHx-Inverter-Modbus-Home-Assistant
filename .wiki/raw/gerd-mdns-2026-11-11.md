# gerd's devices over mDNS, and their web pages, 2026-11-11

Taken by the maintainer's session over a WireGuard VPN into gerd's LAN, with
the owner's leave to publish stand-ins. Multicast does not cross the tunnel, so
every mDNS question was sent **unicast to port 5353 of one device** (a
one-shot query, RFC 6762 section 5.1), with python-zeroconf 0.152.0's message
classes. No Modbus was read for this; nothing was written. Serials are replaced
by the stand-ins of `sungrow_modbus.diagnosis_document.stand_in`; no address.

## What was asked

Of each device: PTR `_services._dns-sd._udp.local.` (the service types it
advertises); PTR of `_http._tcp`, `_https._tcp`, `_modbus._tcp`,
`_device-info._tcp`, `_workstation._tcp`, `_hap._tcp` and `_mqtt._tcp`; PTR of
its reverse name; then TXT of each instance found.

## Answers

| Device | Answers | Service types | Instance | SRV target and port |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WiNet-S | yes | `_http._tcp` only | `WiNet-WebServer._http._tcp.local.` | `SUNGROW<dongle serial>.local.`, port 80 |
| AC22E-01 wallbox, at its own address | yes | `_http._tcp` only | `SUNGROW<wallbox serial>._http._tcp.local.` | `SUNGROW<wallbox serial>.local.`, port 516 |
| SH8.0RT-V112, its own LAN port | no answer to any question | | | |
| the router (FRITZ!Box) | no answer to any question | | | |

No device answered its reverse name. The WiNet-S's A record and two AAAA
records (a link-local and a ULA address) came with the SRV answer; the
wallbox's A record did, no AAAA.

TTLs: PTR and TXT 4500 s, SRV and address records 120 s.

## TXT records

The WiNet-S, one string per line, serials replaced:

```
battery1=2;200;8423;<anon-77256224535>;1;516;SBR096;
ethinfo=;
inverter=1;3598;<anon-00267885816>;1;516;SH8.0RT;1;4;
board=SUNGROW;1;WiNet-S;<anon-68311414753>;1;
```

The wallbox:

```
board=SUNGROW;3;AC22E-01;<anon-41346964423>;1;248;
info=16256;516;1;
```

The stand-ins were computed from the serials the records carry. The
inverter's (`anon-00267885816`) and the wallbox's (`anon-41346964423`) are
those of gerd's committed diagnoses, so the records name the devices those
documents measured. The dongle's and the battery's have not appeared in a
document before.

Numbers in the records, as decimal and hex: 3598 = 0x0E0E; 8423 = 0x20E7;
16256 = 0x3F80.
